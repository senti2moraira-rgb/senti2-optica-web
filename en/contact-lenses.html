By replacement

How often they are changed

The more often a lens is replaced, the better the hygiene and comfort. We advise you based on how many days you wear them.

  • Daily disposables

    A fresh pair every day: no solutions, no case. The most comfortable and hygienic option, ideal for sport, travel or occasional wear.

  • Two-weekly

    Replaced every two weeks. A good balance between freshness and price for everyday wearers.

  • Monthly

    One lens a month, cleaned daily. The most economical for continuous wear, in materials that let plenty of oxygen through.

  • Yearly

    Conventional long-life lenses, made to measure for prescriptions or shapes not available in disposables.

By visual need

According to your prescription

Each visual defect has its own lens design. During the fitting we measure your eye and try the lens that suits it best.

  • Spherical: short and long sight

    The classic lens, for clear distance or near vision. Available in every replacement schedule.

  • Toric: astigmatism

    Designed to stay stable on the eye and correct astigmatism sharply. Now available as dailies, two-weeklies and monthlies.

  • Multifocal: presbyopia

    From around 40 or 45, to see far and near without glasses. They need a careful fitting, which we do without rush.

  • Myopia control for children

    Soft lenses designed to slow down the progression of short-sightedness in children and teenagers, with regular check-ups.

  • For dry eyes

    Materials and wetting agents designed for eyes that dry out with screens, air conditioning or wind. Best as dailies.

Specialist lenses

For specific needs and tastes

  • Rigid gas permeable

    Small, hard lenses that give very sharp vision and last for years. Suitable for high astigmatism or irregular corneas. We fit them with trials and follow-up.

  • Coloured

    Change or enhance the colour of your iris with a natural result. Available with prescription.

  • Costume lenses

    For Halloween, carnival or parties: vampire, cat or completely white eyes. See the seven models we stock at €15 a pair (in Spanish).

  • Solutions and care

    Multipurpose solutions, peroxide, wetting drops and cases. We tell you which suits your lenses and your eyes.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I wear contact lenses with astigmatism?

Yes. Toric lenses correct astigmatism and come as dailies, two-weeklies and monthlies.

And with presbyopia?

Yes too: multifocal lenses let you see far and near. The fitting takes a little longer, which is why we do it without rush.

How many hours a day can I wear them?

It depends on the material and your eye; usually between 8 and 12 hours. Never sleep in them unless we have told you that you can.

From what age can children wear lenses?

There is no fixed age: it depends on maturity and hygiene. Many children wear them from 8 or 10, especially for sport or myopia control.

Can I buy lenses online with your prescription?

You can, but a lens is not just a prescription: it is a material, a curvature and a diameter fitted to your eye. Here we give you the lens we have tried on you and follow you up.
